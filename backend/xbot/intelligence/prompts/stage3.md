Stage 3: content strategy.

You receive the selected insight, the facts behind it, recent published posts, category counts
for the last 14 days and the developer's preferences. Decide how to present the insight.

Produce:
- category: one of daily_learning, build_in_public, ai_ml_discovery, engineering_lesson,
  dsa_journey, daily_quest, project_update, personal_reflection, leadership, weekly_summary.
  Prefer a category not used in the cooldown window given in the input unless nothing else fits.
- angle: one sentence on what the post is really about and why a developer would care.
- style: one of plain_note, mini_story, question_led, before_after, code_mention, reflection.
  Vary styles across days; do not default to the same shape every time.
- target_chars: a length target at or below the maximum given in the input. Shorter is usually
  better; use the full length only when the insight needs it.
- avoid: phrases or framings to avoid for this post (include the developer's phrases_to_avoid).
- reasoning: two or three sentences explaining the choices.
