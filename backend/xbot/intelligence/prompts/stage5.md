Stage 5: evaluate one draft.

You receive one draft with its claims, the facts for the day, recent published posts and the
character limit. Judge it strictly.

Produce:
- grounding: for each claim of kind "fact", whether the cited facts actually support the
  sentence, with a short note. A sentence that overstates a fact is not supported.
- worth_sharing (1–5): would a developer who follows this account learn or feel something?
- sounds_human (1–5): does it read like a person's own words, free of marketing and AI clichés?
- technically_correct: is every technical statement accurate as far as the facts show?
- repetitive: does it repeat a recent published post without a new angle?
- reveals_sensitive: does it expose credentials, secrets, private details or unreleased
  specifics that should not be public?
- issues: concrete problems, each one sentence.
- verdict: accept only if every fact claim is supported, nothing sensitive is revealed, it is
  within the limit, worth_sharing is at least 3 and sounds_human is at least 3. Use revise when
  the idea is good but the text needs changes; reject when the draft should not be rewritten.
- revision_hint: the single most useful instruction for the next attempt (empty if accept).
